## Ingredients

- 1 cup water
- 1 cup vinegar (min 5% acidity)
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp salt
- Thinly sliced veggies

## Directions

1. Heat up ingredients until all dissolved.
1. Let cool slightly.
1. Pour brine over veggies in a heat resistant container (e.g. mason jar).
1. Let cool completely and store in fridge for up to three weeks.
